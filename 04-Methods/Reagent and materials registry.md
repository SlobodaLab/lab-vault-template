# Reagent & Materials Registry

Cross-project record of physical reagents, primers, and key materials. One row per item, written once when received or opened, reused across experiments and projects.

**What goes here:** the identity of a reagent, primer, or stock; vendor, catalogue number, lot, expiry, where it lives.
**What does not:** what happened on a given prep day. Batch records and protocols live in lab notebook entries (`01-Daily Notes/Lab Notebook/`).

**Provenance** is `verified` (read off the container) or `unknown` (reagents from someone else, or unlabeled stocks). Never leave it blank; an empty cell later reads as fact.
**Recorded** is the date the row was last checked against the physical item.
